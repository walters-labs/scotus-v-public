[Decision source](https://www.law.cornell.edu/supct/html/08-205.ZS.html)

### Independent corporate and union spending

**expands · contextual**

Independent expenditures differ from donations directly to candidates; the ruling did not legalize unrestricted direct corporate donations.

The survey contrasts contribution limits with freedom to support campaigns. It does not isolate independent expenditures; no agreement rate with the holding can be calculated.

## McCutcheon v. Federal Election Commission (2014-04-02)

[Decision source](https://www.law.cornell.edu/supremecourt/text/12-536)

### Aggregate donor limits

**expands · contextual**

The Court struck down the total a donor could contribute across candidates and committees.

Per-recipient base limits were not struck down. Four justices joined Roberts’s reasoning; Thomas supplied the fifth vote with a separate rationale.

The poll addresses candidate fundraising and spending, not the aggregate donor cap. This is broad regulatory context and a weak match to the legal question.

Source locator: Roberts opinion, Parts II–IV; Thomas concurrence.
