[Decision source](https://www.law.cornell.edu/supremecourt/text/20-843)

Direction refers to firearm rights, not liberal ideology. The 52.6% point estimate is a narrow lead, not a significance finding.

### Special-need requirement for public carry

**expands · direct**

Invalidated New York’s special-need condition for a public-carry license.

Protects public carry for ordinary self-defense; does not invalidate all objective licensing requirements.

The poll addresses this named licensing condition. Its simpler self-protection wording does not measure approval of the historical-tradition test.

## United States v. Rahimi (2024-06-21)

[Decision source](https://www.law.cornell.edu/supremecourt/text/22-915)

Do not turn a broad group label in the poll into a conclusion about every restraining order or lifetime disarmament.

### Temporary disarmament after a judicial threat finding

**maintains_restriction · contextual**

Allowed temporary disarmament following a court finding of a credible physical threat.

Facial challenge to the restraining-order statute; the Court’s conclusion is narrower than a ban on everyone described as an abuser.

The poll names domestic abusers broadly but omits the judicial finding and temporary duration. Its 74.0% estimate is related context, not exact approval of all statutory conditions.

Source locator: Roberts majority opinion Part II and concluding paragraph.

## Limits

- Explicit source-based legal coding is an input, not an automated legal classifier.
- Contextual matches do not measure approval of the specific holding.
- Mixed and unresolved components retain their labels; no scalar direction is inferred.
- No pooling, confidence intervals, causal claims, or prediction.
- Published toplines, not independently reweighted microdata; exact question wording is in the source PDF.
- These deliberately chosen examples are not a representative sample. Narrow percentage leads are not significance claims.
- Direct match denotes the stated policy component, not endorsement of every rationale or factual qualification.
