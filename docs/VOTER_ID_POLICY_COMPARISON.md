## Crawford v. Marion County Election Board (2008-04-28)

[Decision source](https://www.law.cornell.edu/supct/html/07-21.ZS.html)

Six justices agreed on the judgment, not one rationale. Stevens, Roberts and Kennedy relied on the record and the broad challenge; Scalia, Thomas and Alito concurred separately. Souter and Ginsburg dissented, as did Breyer separately. Public policy support does not settle the constitutional question.

### Photo identification for in-person voting

**maintains_restriction · contextual**

Indiana’s photo-ID requirement remained in place after the Court rejected the facial challenge.

The judgment did not establish that every voter-ID law or every application of this law is constitutional.

The poll asks about photo ID generally. It does not specify Indiana’s exemptions, provisional-ballot process, or burdens on voters who lack qualifying identification.

Source locator: Stevens opinion, Parts III–IV; Scalia concurrence in the judgment; syllabus identifies the separate opinions.
