What the surveys measure

Pew’s March–April 2023 question describes using race and ethnicity in selective-college admissions to increase diversity. It is a closer policy match than the broader CES and VOTER questions.

What the cases decide

The Court reviewed particular university programs. Fisher’s 2013 remand did not settle their validity; the 2016 and 2023 rulings addressed different programs and records.
