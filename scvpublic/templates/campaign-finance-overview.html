Campaign finance · A closer look

Which money, which limit?

Independent advertising, direct donations, aggregate donor caps and disclosure are different legal questions. The earlier surveys provide broad context rather than direct tests of the holdings.

The public-policy questions

Gallup asked about contribution limits versus free speech in 2009, and limits on candidates’ fundraising and spending in 2013.

The legal questions

Citizens United protected independent corporate and union spending while preserving disclosure requirements. McCutcheon struck down aggregate donor limits, not per-recipient caps.

This is one of the thinner comparisons. Neither question isolates the disputed restriction. The 2009 chart leaves 7% unreported; that remainder is shown without guessing its meaning. The 2013 total is 101% because of published rounding.
