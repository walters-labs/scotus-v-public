Support for execution—and exceptions to it?

General support for the death penalty does not tell us whether people support executing juveniles or people with intellectual disability.

What the surveys measure

GSS asks about the death penalty for murder convictions. That broadly matches Gregg’s permissibility question.

What the cases decide

Atkins and Roper concern exemptions for particular groups. Their survey cards show general background only, not opposition to those exemptions.

The exemption comparisons still need targeted questions. These three observations are not presented as a continuous trend.
