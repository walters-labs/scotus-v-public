Handgun rights · A closer look

An individual right—and the limits of the comparison

Before Heller, Gallup asked the constitutional question at the heart of the case: does the Second Amendment protect an individual right or only militia members? That gives us a closer comparison than general preferences about gun control.

Two different survey questions

Gallup asked about banning possession, with exceptions for police and other authorized people. Pew asked about banning sales. Their responses appear separately below.

Two different legal steps

Heller protected keeping a handgun at home in D.C. McDonald extended that constitutional protection to state and local governments. Neither decision eliminated all gun regulation.

Where the comparison stops

The ban questions provide additional policy context. Neither they nor the individual-right question measures support for extending the right to state governments in McDonald, or for Heller’s separate storage ruling. That component remains explicitly unmeasured.

These observations are historical context, not a continuous trend. The same 2008 Pew poll appears under both cases; it is not two independent measurements.
