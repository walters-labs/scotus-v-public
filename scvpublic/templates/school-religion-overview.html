School religion · A closer look

Who is speaking—the school or a private group?

Two rulings distinguish school-sponsored prayer from religious expression in an after-school forum. A single pro-religion or anti-religion score would hide that distinction.

The public-policy questions

Gallup asked about graduation prayer in 1999 and after-hours religious groups in 2001, before the respective rulings. These are different questions, not a trend.

The legal questions

Santa Fe concerned a school prayer policy at football games. Good News Club concerned equal access for a private religious club.

Graduation differs from football; student groups differ from an adult-led club. These are contextual comparisons, not approval ratings for either decision. This selected history does not describe every later change in school-religion law.
