Voter ID · A closer look

Should voters have to show photo identification?

A large majority supported the general requirement before Crawford. The Court left Indiana’s law in place, but the poll did not ask about that law’s particular rules.

What the Court decided

In April 2008, a 6–3 judgment rejected the challenge to Indiana’s law as a whole. The six justices did not share a single rationale.

General support is not approval of every rule

The poll and the outcome point toward allowing photo ID. That does not tell us whether respondents accepted Indiana’s particular requirements or the burdens they imposed. This is one historical snapshot, not a trend or a measure of overall agreement on voting rights.
